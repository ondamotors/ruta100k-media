## Slide 2 · Las 3 señales

URL : https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/?lang=en · date affichée : June 18, 2020

- "Recommendations are based on a number of factors, including things like:"
- "User interactions such as the videos you like or share, accounts you follow, comments you post, and content you create."
- "Video information, which might include details like captions, sounds, and hashtags."
- "Device and account settings like your language preference, country setting, and device type."
- "These factors are included to make sure the system is optimized for performance, but they receive lower weight in the recommendation system relative to other data points we measure since users don't actively express these as preferences."

## ELITE

**Que lo vean hasta el final**
URL : https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/?lang=en · June 18, 2020
- "All these factors are processed by our recommendation system and weighted based on their value to a user."
- "A strong indicator of interest, such as whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end, would receive greater weight than a weak indicator, such as whether the video's viewer and creator are both in the same country."

**Que no lo salten**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system · pas de date affichée
- "Predictions estimate the likelihood you will interact with a given video; for example, the probability of liking, sharing, commenting, or skipping a given video."
- "The system predicts a variety of interactions, including your likelihood to:" … "Spend a certain amount of time viewing a video." … "Finish, skip, or favorite a video."

**Que lo compartan, comenten o guarden**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system · pas de date affichée
- "Like, share, comment on, or mark a video as 'Not Interested.'"
- "Finish, skip, or favorite a video."
- "By combining prediction scores for each of these interactions, we can arrive at an overall score for each video, with higher scores signifying a closer match to your preferences."

## SOLID

**Content Check Lite**
URL : https://newsroom.tiktok.com/new-tools-for-creators?lang=en · July 30, 2025
- "The first feature is Content Check Lite, which is rolling out through TikTok Studio on the web to start."
- "This feature allows creators to check whether their content may be eligible for recommendation in the For You feed and make any necessary changes before they upload it."

**Revisar tus analíticas (y apelar)**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines (section Enforcement, Notice and Appeals) · lu dans les versions 2025 et 2026 déposées
- "If your content is made ineligible for the FYF or otherwise restricted, this information will appear in the TikTok analytics tool."
- "If your account was banned, or your content was removed, made ineligible for the FYF, or otherwise restricted, and you believe this was an error, you may appeal the decision."

**Etiquetar el contenido comercial**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/regulated-commercial-activities · Released April 17, 2024 / Effective May 17, 2024
- "If you market a business, product, or service you must disclose it using the content disclosure setting."
- "Marketing content is ineligible for the FYF if it is not disclosed using the content disclosure setting."
Version 2025 (dépôt Californie) : "If commercial content isn't disclosed using the content disclosure setting, it will be ineligible for the FYF."
Version US 2026 (dépôt New York) : "If you're posting commercial content on TikTok, you must clearly disclose it using the content disclosure setting." / "If we find commercial content that hasn't been properly disclosed, we may apply the content disclosure setting ourselves or remove it from the FYF."
D'où la formulation « puede quedar fuera del Para ti », vraie pour les deux versions.

## OKAY

**Hashtags**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system · pas de date affichée
- "In addition to signals you create by interacting with content, we also take into account various signals about the video, including:" … "The video's soundtrack." … "The length of the video." … "The hashtags associated with the video."
Aucune page lue ne donne de poids aux hashtags, d'où « No dice cuánto pesan ».

**Sonidos en tendencia**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system
- "The video's soundtrack." (signal vidéo)
- "Content similarity measures how different consecutive videos in your For You feed are."
- "If some of the videos that the system has chosen as top-ranked for you are too similar to each other – for example, they use the same sound – we replace them with others to ensure a greater variety in the content you see."
URL : https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/?lang=en · June 18, 2020
- "For example, your For You feed generally won't show two videos in a row made with the same sound or by the same creator."

## LIMIT

**Tener muchos seguidores**
URL : https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/?lang=en · June 18, 2020
- "While a video is likely to receive more views if posted by an account that has more followers, by virtue of that account having built up a larger follower base, neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors in the recommendation system."

## AVOID

**Prometer like por like**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity (Deceptive Behaviors and Fake Engagement) · versions 2025 et 2026 déposées
- "Content that tries to manipulate people into giving gifts or inflating likes or follows isn't eligible for the FYF."
- FYF INELIGIBLE : "Tricking others into increasing engagement, such as:" "“Like-for-like” promises" / "False incentives for gifting or following" / "Misleading claims meant to boost views or popularity"
Version 2024 lue sur tiktok.com/community-guidelines/integrity-authenticity/ : "Content is ineligible for the FYF if it seeks to trick or manipulate others to increase gifts, followers, likes, views, or other engagement metrics."

**Resubir videos sin aportar nada**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity (Unoriginal Content and Intellectual Property Rights) · versions 2025 et 2026 déposées
- "Content is also ineligible for the FYF if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new."
- FYF INELIGIBLE : "Reused or unoriginal content posted without creative edits, such as clips that show someone else's watermark or logo"
Version 2024 lue sur tiktok.com : "Content is ineligible for the FYF if it involves unoriginal or reproduced content that does not have any new or creative changes."

**Comprar seguidores o likes**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity · versions 2025 et 2026 déposées
- "That's why we don't allow accounts that mislead or try to manipulate our platform, or the trade of services that artificially boost engagement or trick the recommendation system."
- NOT ALLOWED : "Buying or selling followers or engagement for financial gain" / "Trading, marketing, or providing access to services that artificially increase engagement, such as:"
- "If we detect accounts or content with inauthentic metrics, we'll remove fake likes, followers, or other inflated signals."
Version 2024 lue sur tiktok.com : "If we become aware of accounts or content with inauthentically inflated metrics, we will remove the associated fake followers or likes."

**Llenar tu cuenta de contenido no apto**
URL : https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features · versions 2025 et 2026 déposées
- "Sometimes, accounts that don't break the rules still post a lot of content that's ineligible for the FYF."
- "In those cases, we may make the account and its content ineligible for the FYF and harder to find."
Version 2024 lue sur tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features : "Repeatedly posting content that is allowed on our platform but ineligible for the FYF may lead to the account and its content being ineligible for recommendation, including being ineligible for the FYF and harder to find in search."
URL : https://newsroom.tiktok.com/cgupdate?lang=en-ZA · April 22, 2024
- "We're introducing a policy to make an entire account temporarily ineligible for recommendation in the For You feed if a creator repeatedly posts content that goes against our For You feed standards."
- "Their account and content will be harder to find in search."
- "We will notify creators when their accounts have been restricted in this way, and they will be able to appeal."

## Vérifié mais écarté

- Clips de faible qualité, « short clips made from GIFs only » (FYF INELIGIBLE, Normas 2025 et 2026) : écarté faute de place.
- Creator Search Insights (Newsroom, 13/03/2024, « available in select regions ») : concerne la recherche, pas le Para ti.
- Account Check (Newsroom, 22/04/2024, audit du compte et des 30 derniers posts) : disponibilité actuelle non vérifiable, remplacé par la règle des analytics des Normas en vigueur.

## Non vérifiable, donc absent

- Creator Academy, article « TikTok originality policy » et page support « How TikTok recommends content » : pages non lisibles (robots.txt / accès refusé), rien n'en est tiré.
- Aucune source officielle lue ne parle d'accroche dans les premières secondes, de fréquence ou d'heure de publication, d'avantage des vidéos longues ou des mots-clés pour le Para ti : rien de tout ça dans le carrousel.

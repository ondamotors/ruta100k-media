## Most surprising verifiable facts for a Spanish-speaking creator audience

1. Reposting now cuts non-follower reach in every format: since 30 Apr 2026 the aggregator rule covers photos and carousels too (TechCrunch), and per Instagram's own guidelines eligibility only returns when the most recent posts over a rolling 30 days are original. Adding a watermark, changing speed or crediting the author does not count as original (4b, 4c).
2. Maximum 5 hashtags per post or reel since Dec 2025, and Mosseri has said hashtags do not increase reach (3a, 3b, secondhand via Social Media Today).
3. A Trial Reel can be set to go to followers automatically only if its views are good within 72 hours, and since Dec 2025 trial reels can be published through the API, so scheduling tools can post them (2a, 2c).
4. There is an official "shadowban check": Account Status tells professional accounts whether they can be recommended to non-followers, shows what is flagged and lets them request a review (6).
